01 Features

One palette for everything you do all day.

Almost everything ships off until you ask for it, so Zappale is exactly as big as you make it.

App launcher

Fuzzy-search every app and open it with a keystroke. Pin favorites, see what is running, quit without the mouse.

Inline calculator

Math, units, currency and dates like days till 9 Apr, answered right in the search field.

560 km to mi → 347.97 mi

Clipboard history

Text, images, files and colors, searchable and pasted straight back where they came from.

Quick actions

Select text in any app, then fix, rewrite, translate or summarize it with one shortcut.

Fix Grammar · Rewrite · Translate

Window management

Halves, thirds, nudges and saved layouts — every move from the keyboard, nothing dragged.

Snippets

Markdown templates with placeholders. Type a keyword anywhere and it expands.

!meeting → Sunday, 20 September

Emoji & symbols

Search the whole set. The ones you actually use float to the top.

Themes & appearance

Light, dark and glass. Follow the system or lock the look you like.

Also in the box

  • File search
  • Quicklinks
  • Floating notes
  • Calendar & meetings
  • System actions
  • Per-app hotkeys

  • Hyper key
  • App uninstaller

  • Custom commands

03 Privacy

Nothing turns on until you do.

A fresh install is a launcher, a calculator and your clipboard. Everything else waits for you to switch it on — and none of it needs an account.

0accounts
0telemetry
0dependencies
<100 MBof memory

AI actions

No rewriting, no summarizing and no history file until you turn it on.

Snippets

The only feature that watches typing. Matching stays on your Mac.

Calendar

Explains what it reads before macOS asks. Events never leave.

Clipboard history

On by default, kept on your Mac for 3 months. Password managers are skipped.

Permissions are asked for the moment a feature needs one — never at launch.

04 Keyboard

Built for the keyboard.

Pick one shortcut to summon the palette. Everything after that is a key away, and keys follow their position, so any layout works.

⌥SpaceSummon the palette, with keys you pick
↵Run the main action
⌘↵Run the second action
⌘KOpen the actions menu
TabLauncher, then clipboard, then calculator
⌘1…0Open favorite 1 to 10
escClear, go back, then close

05 Getting started

Up and running in a minute.

  1. 1

    Download & install

    Grab the latest disk image from releases and drag Zappale to Applications.

  2. 2

    Press ⌥Space

    The palette appears where your cursor is. That is the whole interface.

  3. 3

    Type to launch

    Two letters are usually enough. Everything else can wait until you look for it.

“ No account. No telemetry. No nonsense. ”

the whole privacy policy, more or less

Native, end to end

AppKit and Swift, zero third-party dependencies. It opens before Electron has finished thinking about it.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account, no telemetry, no update pings you did not ask for. AI features stay off until you turn them on.

Open source, MIT

Read every line and build it yourself. The feature set is deliberately small so it stays that way.

Free, and staying free

There is no Pro tier waiting behind a paywall, and there never will be.
